After a technical failure of your warp drive, you found yourself stranded in a strange solar system.

Carefully land your spaceship to extract some fuel. But be careful. Who knows what horrors await in this place.

No 3D APIs were touched in the development of this project.

## Gamepad Controls (recommended)

- **Left Joystick**: Move horizontally
- **Right Joystick**: Look around
- **LT, RT**: Move Up and Down

## Keyboard Controls

- **WASD**: Movement
- **Arrow keys**: Look around
- **Q, E**: Move Up and Down
